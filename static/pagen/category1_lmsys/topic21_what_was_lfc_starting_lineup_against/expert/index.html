Matchday Storyline

LFC vs Brighton: Who Started for the Reds?

On May 19, 2025, Liverpool took on Brighton at the Amex Stadium in a crucial Premier League showdown. Manager Arne Slot surprised fans with four changes, handing Federico Chiesa his first Premier League start. With Salah leading as captain, the Reds went in with a mix of experience and youthful energy.

Formation & Game Plan

The Reds lined up in their trademark 4-3-3 formation, designed for attacking flair and defensive solidity. Bradley and Tsimikas pushed from full-back, Quansah stepped up alongside Konaté, and a lively midfield trio supported a dynamic front line.

Liverpool Starting XI

Here’s how Liverpool kicked off against Brighton:

GK: Alisson Becker

The Brazilian shot-stopper brought his usual calm presence between the posts, ready to command his box and launch quick counterattacks with precise distribution.

DEF: Conor Bradley

The young right-back offered energy and overlapping runs, looking to stretch Brighton’s defense while keeping things tight at the back.

DEF: Ibrahima Konaté

A rock in the heart of defense, Konaté’s strength and pace were key to shutting down Brighton’s attackers and winning aerial duels.

DEF: Jarell Quansah

The academy product continued his breakthrough season, showing composure on the ball and strong positioning alongside Konaté.

DEF: Kostas Tsimikas

The Greek international brought balance on the left side, combining defensive reliability with dangerous crosses going forward.

MID: Ryan Gravenberch

With his physical presence and smooth dribbling, Gravenberch linked defense to attack, helping Liverpool control midfield battles.

MID: Dominik Szoboszlai

Known for his energy and creativity, Szoboszlai pulled the strings in midfield and looked for opportunities to test the keeper from distance.

MID: Harvey Elliott

Always lively, Elliott added quick passing and intelligent movement, keeping Brighton’s defense on alert with his sharp link-up play.

FWD: Mohamed Salah (captain)

Wearing the armband, Salah led from the front with his trademark pace, dribbling, and goal threat—determined to inspire his teammates.

FWD: Cody Gakpo

Operating through the middle, Gakpo’s clever positioning and finishing instincts made him a constant danger inside the box.

FWD: Federico Chiesa

Making his first Premier League start, the Italian forward brought flair and direct running, exciting fans with his pace and cutting-edge play on the wing.

Strength on the Bench

Slot kept plenty of firepower and leadership ready to step in:

Caoimhín Kelleher

Kelleher has proven himself as a reliable goalkeeper whenever called upon. His sharp reflexes and calm presence give Liverpool security between the posts, especially during cup competitions where he has been a standout performer.

Joe Gomez

Versatile and composed, Gomez can play both as a center-back and full-back. His pace and defensive awareness allow him to cover ground quickly, making him an important option in Liverpool’s defensive setup.

Virgil van Dijk

As captain and leader, Van Dijk is the rock of Liverpool’s defense. His dominance in the air, ability to read the game, and leadership qualities inspire confidence throughout the team.

Luis Díaz

Known for his speed and flair, Díaz brings creativity and energy to Liverpool’s attack. His dribbling skills and ability to take on defenders make him a constant threat on the wing.

Darwin Núñez

Núñez is a powerful forward who combines strength with pace. His eye for goal and determination to press defenders make him a valuable asset in Klopp’s high-intensity style of play.

Curtis Jones

A product of Liverpool’s academy, Jones has grown into a confident midfielder. His ball control, vision, and ability to contribute in attack highlight his importance as a young player with big potential.

Andy Robertson

Robertson is one of the most consistent left-backs in the game. His endless stamina, precise crossing, and strong defensive work make him crucial on both ends of the pitch.

Trent Alexander-Arnold

Famed for his attacking play, Trent is Liverpool’s creative outlet from the back. His pinpoint passing and free-kick ability add another dimension to Liverpool’s offense while he continues to develop defensively.

Wataru Endo

Endo provides balance in midfield with his discipline and hard work. His ability to break up play and shield the defense ensures that Liverpool maintains stability when facing tough opponents.

Talking Points

  • Federico Chiesa marked his first Premier League start, thrilling fans with pace and creativity.
