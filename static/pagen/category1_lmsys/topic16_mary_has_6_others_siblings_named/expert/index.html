❓ Riddle: Mary Has 6 Siblings – What's the Last Child's Name?

Mary has 6 others siblings named Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday. That is the name of the last child?

The Riddle

Mary has 6 other siblings named Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday.
What is the name of the last child?

Common Thought Process

🧠 Most people immediately assume:

"If the siblings are named after days of the week, then the missing one must be Sunday."

That seems logical, right? Completing the full set of 7 days makes sense…

But here’s where the trick comes in — let’s look a little closer.

The Trick

The riddle says: "Mary has 6 other siblings…"
That means Mary herself is already one of the children.

So the seven children are:
👉 Mary, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday.

The Answer

🎉 The last child's name is Mary.

Takeaway

This riddle plays on assumptions — our brains jump to patterns (days of the week) instead of carefully reading the wording. Sometimes, the answer is hidden in plain sight.
