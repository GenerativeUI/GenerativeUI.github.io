Category Insight

Understanding what makes something a fruit vs. a vehicle

🍎

What makes something a fruit?

Fruits are parts of plants that usually contain seeds. They're often edible, sweet, and colorful, and grow on plants.

🍎 Apple 🍌 Banana 🍊 Orange
🚗

What makes something a vehicle?

Vehicles are tools or machines that move people or goods from one place to another. Some use engines (like cars), while others use human power (like bicycles).

🚗 Car 🚲 Bicycle 🚌 Bus

Fun Facts & Knowledge Boost

Learn interesting facts about the words from our puzzles

🍎

Apple Facts

  • There are over 7,500 varieties of apples worldwide
  • Apples float in water because 25% of their volume is air
  • The science of apple growing is called pomology
  • Fun fact: "An apple a day keeps the doctor away" dates back to 19th century Wales!
🍊

Orange Facts

  • Botanically, oranges are a special type of berry called a hesperidium
  • Brazil produces 30% of the world's oranges
  • Orange peels contain more vitamin C than the fruit
  • Fun fact: Oranges are the most popular fruit in the world!
🍌

Banana Facts

  • Bananas are berries, and the plant is actually a giant herb
  • Bananas are naturally radioactive due to potassium-40
  • The strings on bananas are called phloem bundles
  • Fun fact: Cavendish bananas may go extinct due to a fungal disease!
🚗

Car Facts

  • The first car was invented in 1886 by Karl Benz
  • There are over 1 billion cars in the world today
  • The average car has 30,000 parts
  • Fun fact: The world's first speeding ticket was issued in 1902 for going 45 mph!
