Step 2 · Make owners feel safe

Quick tips for a strong Rover field

❤ Lead with safety, not fear

Emphasize that these questions help you keep their pet safe and comfortable, not judge their training.

  • Use warm, non‑blaming language.
  • Mention that no detail is “too small”.
⚖ Balance detail with readability

A single, well‑formatted paragraph is easier to skim than a wall of text.

  • Group ideas (health, behavior, routine).
  • Keep sentences medium‑length.
⚡ Show you have a plan

Briefly mention how you’ll use this info: to match their routine, avoid triggers, and react fast in emergencies.

  • Signal that you’ll follow their instructions.
  • Reference vet care preferences clearly.
