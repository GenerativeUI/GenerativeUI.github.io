Step-by-Step Cooking Guide

Follow along with detailed instructions and helpful tips

1

Prep Ingredients

10 min

Wash and dice potatoes into 1-inch cubes. Dice onion and celery. Cook bacon until crispy, then crumble.

💡 Tip: Keep potato pieces uniform for even cooking

2

Sauté Vegetables

5 min

In a large pot, melt butter over medium heat. Add onions and celery, cook until softened, about 5 minutes.

💡 Tip: Don't let the vegetables brown

3

Add Flour

2 min

Sprinkle flour over vegetables and stir constantly for 1-2 minutes to cook out the raw flour taste.

💡 Tip: This creates a roux that will thicken your soup

4

Add Broth & Potatoes

3 min

Gradually whisk in chicken broth to prevent lumps. Add diced potatoes and bring to a boil.

💡 Tip: Whisk constantly while adding broth

5

Simmer

18 min

Reduce heat and simmer until potatoes are tender, about 15-20 minutes. Stir occasionally.

💡 Tip: Test potatoes with a fork for doneness

6

Blend & Finish

5 min

Partially blend soup for desired consistency. Stir in cream, season with salt and pepper. Top with bacon and chives.

💡 Tip: Don't boil after adding cream to prevent curdling
