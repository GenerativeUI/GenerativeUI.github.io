The Marriage Process Timeline

Follow the journey from application to official recognition

1

Apply for License

Visit your county clerk's office with required documents and pay the fee

Required Documents:

  • Valid photo ID
  • Birth certificate
  • Divorce papers (if applicable)
Some states require a waiting period before you can marry.
2

Wedding Ceremony

Exchange vows with your officiant and witnesses present

What happens:

  • Officiant signs the license
  • Witnesses sign (if required)
  • Couple signs the license
Ceremony must occur within the license's validity period (30–90 days in most states).
3

File the License

Your officiant submits the signed license to the county clerk

Timeline:

  • Must usually be filed within a few days after the ceremony (varies by state)
  • Officiant handles the filing
  • License becomes official record
Check local rules—some areas require couples to return the license themselves.
4

Receive Certificate

Receive (or request) your official marriage certificate from the county clerk

What you get:

  • Official proof of marriage
  • Use for name changes
  • Legal documentation
You may need to order official copies from the county clerk for legal use.
Optional
5

Update Your Records

Use your marriage certificate to update official documents and accounts

Documents to update:

  • Driver's license
  • Passport
  • Bank accounts
  • Social Security (if in the U.S.)
This step is optional and depends on whether you're changing your name.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about marriage documents

Important: Laws and timelines may differ depending on your state or country. Always check with your local county clerk or registrar's office for exact requirements.

Can I get married without a marriage license?

No, you cannot legally get married without a marriage license. The license is the legal permission that allows your marriage ceremony to be recognized by the government. Without it, your marriage would not be legally valid.

Do I need both a marriage license and certificate?

Yes, you need both documents. The marriage license gives you permission to marry, while the marriage certificate proves that you are actually married. They serve different purposes in the marriage process.

How long does a marriage license last?

Marriage licenses typically expire after 30-90 days, depending on your state. If you don't get married within this timeframe, you'll need to apply for a new license. Check with your local county clerk for specific expiration periods.

Can I use my marriage certificate for name changes?

Yes, your marriage certificate is the official document you need to change your name legally. You'll need it for updating your Social Security card, driver's license, passport, and other official documents.

What if I lose my marriage certificate?

Don't worry! You can request a replacement copy from the county clerk's office where you were married. There's usually a small fee ($10-$25) for additional copies. It's a good idea to order multiple copies when you first receive your certificate.

How long does it take to get a marriage certificate?

After your officiant files the marriage license, it typically takes 2-6 weeks to receive your marriage certificate in the mail. Some counties offer expedited processing for an additional fee.

Do marriage certificates expire?

No, marriage certificates never expire. Once issued, they are permanent legal documents that remain valid for your entire life. You can use them at any time for legal purposes.

Can I apply for a marriage license online?

Many counties now allow online applications for marriage licenses, though some may still require an in-person visit for document verification. Check with your local county clerk's office for their specific requirements.

Do both partners need to be present to get a marriage license?

In most places, yes, both applicants must appear in person with valid ID. Some exceptions exist for military service or hardship cases, but these require special documentation and approval from the county clerk.

Can a marriage license from one state be used in another?

Usually no — marriage licenses are valid only in the state/county where they are issued. If you plan to get married in a different state, you'll need to apply for a new license in that location.

Is a religious ceremony valid without a marriage license?

It may be valid religiously, but not legally. Without a license, the government won't recognize the marriage. You need both a valid marriage license and a legal ceremony to be legally married.

How much does a marriage license cost?

Typically $25–$100 depending on the county or state. Some areas offer reduced fees for military personnel or senior citizens. Check with your local county clerk for exact pricing and payment methods accepted.

Do I need witnesses at my wedding?

Many states require 1–2 witnesses to sign the marriage license, while some do not. Witnesses must be of legal age and present during the ceremony. Check your local laws to confirm witness requirements.
