🚆 What's the Best Way to Travel from Tel Aviv to Jerusalem?

Car, bus, train, or even plane? Here's a clear guide comparing all your options for the 60 km journey between Israel's two major cities.

👉 Compare Travel Options

Distance & Context

60 km

Distance

45-75 min

Travel Time

The journey between Tel Aviv and Jerusalem is one of the most traveled routes in Israel. Whether you're a tourist exploring the country's highlights or a local commuting for work, understanding the best transportation option for your needs is essential.

The distance is approximately 60 kilometers (37 miles), with travel times varying based on mode of transport, time of day, and traffic conditions.

Tel Aviv → Jerusalem Route

Travel Options

🚆

Train

Modern, comfortable rail service between city centers

Time: 35-45 minutes
Cost: 20 NIS ($5.50).
Pros: Fast, reliable, scenic views
Cons: Limited operating hours on weekends
🚌

Bus

Frequent bus service from central stations

Time: 45-75 minutes
Cost: 16 NIS ($4.50).
Pros: Cheap, runs 24/7 on weekdays
Cons: Subject to traffic delays
🚗

Car

Private car or taxi via Highway 1

Time: 45-90 minutes
Cost: Gas + parking (often difficult in Jerusalem)
Pros: Flexible, door-to-door
Cons: Traffic jams, parking issues, stress of driving
✈️

Plane

Not practical.

No commercial flights for this short distance.
Verdict: Skip it

Tips & Advice

🎫
Buy Rav-Kav card: Or use mobile apps for tickets.
⚠️
Rush hours: 7–9 am, 4–7 pm → avoid if possible.
✡️
Shabbat: Trains and buses stop from Friday evening – Saturday evening. Plan accordingly.
🚕
Shared taxis (sherut): Operate on Shabbat and can be an alternative.
