Phone Evolution

The first mobile phone call was made in 1973. Since then, phones have evolved from brick-sized devices to sleek smartphones that fit in your pocket!

Tigers can run up to 65 km/h (40 mph)! That's faster than most cars in city traffic. Imagine a tiger trying to catch up with a delivery notification!
