Quick Dinner Ideas with Cod, Tomatoes, Peppers, Mozzarella, and Arugula

The Challenge

You open your fridge and pantry and find yourself with a specific set of ingredients: frozen cod filets, tomatoes, lemons, bell peppers, fresh mozzarella cheese, and arugula. The question is: what can you make for dinner in under 30 minutes using only these items?

Step 1: Clarify Your Pantry

Before jumping into recipes, it’s important to know exactly what’s available. The listed ingredients give us a lot to work with, but a few extras may be necessary to bring the dishes together. For example:

  • Do you have olive oil or butter for cooking the fish?
  • Do you have salt, pepper, or basic spices to season?
  • Do you have garlic or onions for extra flavor?

If you confirm these, we can unlock more complete, flavorful options. If not, we’ll keep it as minimal as possible with what you do have.

Step 2: Core Recipe Ideas

1. Lemon-Cod with Arugula Salad

Pan-sear or bake the cod with lemon juice, then serve it over a bed of arugula, fresh mozzarella slices, and tomato wedges. Bell peppers can be roasted quickly in the oven or sautéed in a pan for sweetness.

2. Mediterranean Cod Tray Bake

Place cod, tomatoes, and bell peppers on a baking sheet. Drizzle with lemon juice (and oil if available), season lightly, then bake until the fish is cooked through. Top with mozzarella and serve with fresh arugula on the side.

3. Warm Cod & Veggie Salad

Cook cod simply in a pan or oven, then slice and toss with roasted tomatoes, bell peppers, mozzarella chunks, lemon juice, and fresh arugula. A quick, hearty salad that doubles as a main dish.

Step 3: Time-Saving Tips

  • Since the cod is frozen, make sure to thaw it quickly in cold water for 15–20 minutes before cooking.
  • Cut vegetables into small pieces to reduce cooking time.
  • Fresh mozzarella is soft, so add it at the end to avoid melting it into a puddle.

Conclusion

With just a few ingredients and less than half an hour, you can prepare a wholesome, Mediterranean-inspired meal. Once you confirm if you have oil, spices, or garlic, we can refine these into exact, step-by-step recipes that make the most of what’s in your kitchen.
