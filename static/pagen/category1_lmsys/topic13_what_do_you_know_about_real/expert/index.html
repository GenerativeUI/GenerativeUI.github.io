What is Real Estate?

Real estate refers to land and any permanent structures on it — like houses, buildings, or commercial spaces.

It includes residential, commercial, industrial, and land properties.

Real estate is both a place to live and an investment asset.

Types of Real Estate

Residential

Homes, apartments, condos, vacation houses.

Commercial

Offices, malls, shops, hotels.

Industrial

Warehouses, factories, distribution centers.

Land

Farms, undeveloped land, plots.

Why is Real Estate Important?

Builds wealth through appreciation and rental income.

Provides shelter and shapes communities.

Acts as a long-term investment to hedge against inflation.

How People Make Money in Real Estate

Buying & Selling (Flipping)

Purchase low, sell high.

Renting/Leasing

Earn steady monthly income.

REITs

Invest without owning physical property.

Development

Build new projects or improve existing ones.

Test Your Knowledge

Which type of property is best for beginners to invest in?

Answer: For beginners, residential real estate is often the best starting point. It's generally easier to understand, financing is more readily available, and there's typically a larger pool of potential tenants or buyers compared to commercial properties.
