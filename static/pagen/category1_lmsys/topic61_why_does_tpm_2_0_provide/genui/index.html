Why does TPM 2.0 split control into four distinct areas? Explore the architecture of trust, privacy, and ownership.

lightbulb The Short Answer

TPM 2.0 uses Multiple Hierarchies to solve a "too many cooks" problem. In a modern computer, different entities need to store secrets, but they shouldn't trust each other:

  • domain The Manufacturer (OEM) needs to secure the boot process (BIOS/UEFI) regardless of who owns the laptop.
  • person The User/Admin needs to store personal keys (BitLocker) and be able to wipe them easily without bricking the device.
  • badge The Privacy Officer needs to prove the device's hardware identity without exposing user data.
