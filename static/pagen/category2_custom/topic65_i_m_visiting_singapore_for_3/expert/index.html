September Survival Intel

Singapore is efficient, but September brings specific challenges you need to prepare for.

The F1 Factor

September is Grand Prix season. The Marina Bay street circuit causes major road closures around Suntec and MBS.

Avoid taxis in the CBD. Use the MRT (Subway) to get to your conference on time.

Heat & Haze

Expect 31°C (88°F) with 85% humidity. You will sweat instantly outside. September can also bring "Sumatra Squalls" (sudden storms).

  • Pack a foldable umbrella.
  • Wear breathable fabrics (linen/cotton).

Local Etiquette

"Choping": If you see a packet of tissue on a table at a food court, that seat is taken. Do not sit there.

Transport

MRT (Subway): Use your contactless Visa/Mastercard. No ticket needed.

Grab: Download the app. Uber does not exist here.

Payments

Cash is king at old Hawker Centers. Cards work everywhere else (Malls, Taxis).

Safety

Singapore is extremely safe. You can walk alone at 3 AM anywhere.

Connectivity

Buy an eSIM (Airalo/Singtel) before arrival. Airport WiFi requires a kiosk scan.
