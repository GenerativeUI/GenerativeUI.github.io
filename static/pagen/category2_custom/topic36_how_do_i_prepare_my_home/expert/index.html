Stay Safe When an Earthquake Strikes

When shaking begins, act fast. The “Drop, Cover, Hold On” technique protects you from falling objects—your biggest risk during a quake.

1

Drop

Drop onto hands and knees so you’re stable.

2

Cover

Protect your head and take cover under sturdy furniture.

3

Hold On

Hold on until the shaking fully stops.

Earthquake Safety Checklist

Make your home safer in minutes. Go through this checklist to reduce hazards before an earthquake occurs.

Secure Heavy Furniture

Anchor bookshelves, cabinets & TVs

Emergency Exits

Keep pathways clear at all times

Child Safety

Remove falling hazards from kids’ rooms

Electrical Safety

Keep cords organized & out of the way

Emergency Lights

Place flashlights in easy-to-reach spots

Fasten Wall Hangings

Use proper hooks for mirrors & frames

Prepare Your Emergency Go-Bag

A well-packed emergency kit helps you stay safe if you must leave your home quickly after an earthquake. Keep it in an easy-to-reach place and make sure all family members know where it is.

Create a Family Emergency Plan

Earthquakes can disrupt communication, so planning ahead is essential. Make sure everyone in your home knows what to do, where to go, and how to stay connected.

Contact List

Save important numbers and keep copies accessible.

Meeting Spot

Choose a safe outdoor place for everyone to gather.

Communication Plan

Decide how you’ll reach each other if phones fail.

Pet Plan
