Letters to Theo

Vincent wrote over 800 letters to his brother Theo. These excerpts reveal his thoughts on art, color, and the struggles behind each masterpiece.

"I often think that the night is more alive and more richly colored than the day."
— Letter to Theo, September 1888
"What would life be if we had no courage to attempt anything?"
— Letter to Theo, December 1881
"I put my heart and soul into my work, and have lost my mind in the process."
— Letter to Theo, July 1890
"Great things are not done by impulse, but by a series of small things brought together."
— Letter to Theo, October 1882
"I dream my painting and I paint my dream."
— Letter to Theo, 1888
