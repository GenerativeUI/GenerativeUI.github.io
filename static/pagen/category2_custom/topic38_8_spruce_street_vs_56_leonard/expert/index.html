About the Buildings

A quick look at the design, style, and purpose of each tower.

8 Spruce Street

  • Completed: 2011
  • Height: 870 ft
  • Architect: Frank Gehry
  • Known for: Wavy stainless-steel curves
  • Category: Luxury residential

56 Leonard Street

  • Completed: 2017
  • Height: 821 ft
  • Architect: Herzog & de Meuron
  • Known for: Jenga-style stacked design
  • Category: Ultra-luxury residential

    • Architecture & Design

    Both skyscrapers stand out in NYC for their bold and innovative architectural approaches.

    8 Spruce Street

    • Sculptural, fluid form
    • Reflective metal waves
    • Soft, organic curves
    • Gehry’s signature style: movement + texture

    56 Leonard Street

    • Floating stacked cubes
    • Modern, geometric, sharp edges
    • Minimalist glass look
    • Strong visual contrast in skyline
