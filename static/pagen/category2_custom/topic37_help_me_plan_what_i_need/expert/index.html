Must-Have Furniture for Your Baby’s Room

Your newborn’s room doesn’t need to be crowded. Start with essential, safe furniture that supports sleep, feeding, and storage.

Furniture to Mention:

  • Crib or bassinet
  • Changing table
  • Nursing chair
  • Storage shelves or dresser
  • Soft rug

Baby Room Safety Checklist

Your baby’s bedroom should be safe, calm, and hazard-free. A few simple steps can make the room secure and comfortable for everyday use. Use this checklist to make sure your newborn’s space is ready.

Crib meets current safety standards

Maintain a safe room temperature

Baby monitor installed properly

Electrical cords kept out of reach

Soft, indirect lighting

Use breathable bedding only

Non-toxic materials & paint

Furniture anchored to walls
