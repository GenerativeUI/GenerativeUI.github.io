Home Arenas

United Center

Chicago, Illinois

Also Home To Chicago Blackhawks (NHL)
Status Largest NBA Arena

The United Center is the largest arena in the NBA by capacity. It replaced the Chicago Stadium and has hosted multiple Bulls championships. The arena features the famous "Madhouse on Madison" atmosphere.

Amway Center

Orlando, Florida

Also Home To Orlando Predators (AFL)
Status State-of-the-Art Facility

The Amway Center replaced the Amway Arena. It's a state-of-the-art facility featuring advanced technology and fan amenities. The arena has hosted numerous concerts and events in addition to Magic games.
