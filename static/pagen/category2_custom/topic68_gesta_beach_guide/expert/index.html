Etiquette

The "Golden Rules"

Do's

  • 1. Sit on a towel. Hygiene is mandatory on benches/sauna.
  • 2. Bring cash/card. The sauna & cafe (weather permitting) take payments.
  • 3. Respect distance. The lawn is huge, don't crowd others.

Strictly Forbidden

  • 1. NO CAMERAS. Protect privacy at all costs.
  • 2. Avoid wooded areas. Stay in the open main areas for safety.
  • 3. No glass bottles. Bare feet need protection.
