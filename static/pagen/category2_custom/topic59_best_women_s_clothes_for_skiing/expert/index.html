Common Errors

Mistakes That Ruin
Your Ski Day

Even premium ski clothing won’t perform well if worn incorrectly. Avoid these common mistakes to stay warm, dry, and comfortable from the first chairlift to the last run.

Wearing Cotton Layers

Cotton traps moisture and loses heat quickly, leaving you cold once you start sweating.

Better option: Merino wool or synthetic base layers that wick moisture and retain warmth.

Over-Layering

Too many layers restrict movement and cause overheating, leading to sweat buildup and discomfort.

Tip: Use fewer breathable layers with proper ventilation instead of bulky stacks.

Ignoring Wind Chill

Wind can drastically lower perceived temperature, especially on chairlifts and exposed ridgelines.

Essential: A windproof outer shell with sealed seams to block cold air.

Poor Hand Protection

Cold hands ruin comfort fast and reduce control over poles and bindings.

Best choice: Insulated, waterproof gloves or mittens designed for alpine conditions.
