Essential Travel Guide

Most Western citizens (US, EU, UK, Canada) do not need visas for tourism in Argentina, Peru, Chile, Colombia, or Brazil. Note: Bolivia may require a visa on arrival for US citizens ($160). Always check current regulations before travel.
  • Altitude: Acclimatize in Cusco or La Paz for 2 days before strenuous hikes.
  • Water: Drink bottled or purified water only.
  • Vaccines: Yellow Fever is recommended for the Amazon. Routine vaccines should be up to date.
  • Safety: Use Uber/Cabify in cities instead of street taxis at night. Keep valuables minimal.

SIM Cards: Buy a local SIM (Claro/Movistar/Entel) or use an Airalo eSim for convenience.

Cash vs Card: Credit cards are widely accepted in major cities. Carry cash (USD) to exchange for small towns in Argentina (Blue Dollar rate) and Bolivia (Cash is king).
