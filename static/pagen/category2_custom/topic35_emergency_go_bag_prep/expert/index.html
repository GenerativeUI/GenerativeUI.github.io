WHAT IS AN EMERGENCY GO-BAG

An emergency go-bag is a backpack filled with essential items you’ll need during a disaster. It helps you evacuate quickly and stay safe for at least 72 hours. A good go-bag ensures you have food, water, first aid, tools, and important documents ready to grab anytime.
