The Fog Strategy

SF Parent Hack: The "Fog Layer" Strategy

San Francisco weather is a collection of microclimates. It might be 72°F in JapanTown, but drop to 55°F at the Golden Gate Bridge within minutes.

The Hack: Always keep a "Go-Bag" in the stroller with a windbreaker for both kids. Precisely at 4 PM, the fog creates "wind tunnels" between buildings.
Pro-Move: Don't trust general apps. Use 'Mr. Chilly' for neighborhood-specific temps. January air is damp, so fleece layers are essential.
