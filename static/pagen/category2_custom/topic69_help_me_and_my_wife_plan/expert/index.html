Pre-Trip Essentials

Visit Japan Web

Mandatory for entry. Register online to generate your Immigration & Customs QR codes before you board the plane.

Cash is King

Japan is still cash-heavy. Many ticket machines and street stalls (Osaka) only take yen. 7-Eleven ATMs are your best friend.

eSim Connectivity

Reliable data is crucial for Google Maps. Pre-buying an eSim (Ubigi or Airalo) is often cheaper than Pocket Wifi rental.

Suica / Pasmo

The JR Pass has increased in price. For this route, an IC Card (Welcome Suica) on your phone is the smartest way to pay.
