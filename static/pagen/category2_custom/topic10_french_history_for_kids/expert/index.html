Must-See Places in France!

France is full of amazing buildings and places that tell stories from the past. Here are three famous landmarks every kid should know!

The Eiffel Tower

Built in 1889, the Eiffel Tower is one of the most famous structures in the world. Millions of people visit it every year!

Palace of Versailles

A giant golden palace where French kings once lived! It has huge gardens and beautiful rooms full of history.

Mont Saint-Michel

A magical island with a giant castle on top! When the tide rises, the island looks like it’s floating on water.
