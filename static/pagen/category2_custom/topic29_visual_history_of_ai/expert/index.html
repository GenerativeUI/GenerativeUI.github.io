How Neural Networks Learn

Neural networks consist of layers of interconnected artificial neurons. Each connection has a weight that determines how much influence one neuron has on another. During forward propagation, signals flow from input through hidden layers to output.

Training adjusts these weights through backpropagation: the network compares its output to the desired result, calculates the error, and updates weights to minimize this error. Over many training iterations (epochs), the network learns to recognize patterns and make accurate predictions.
