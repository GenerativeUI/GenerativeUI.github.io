Myth busters

Common phone myths

These myths cause bad purchases. Tap to expand.

Sensor size, lens quality, stabilization, and processing matter more. A well-tuned camera system often beats a high-megapixel spec.
Sustained performance (heat control) and software optimization decide day-to-day smoothness more than peak benchmarks.
Value phones can be “best” if your priorities are battery, reliability, and decent cameras—without premium extras you won’t use.
Windows Phone is discontinued and not part of current mainstream smartphone choices. It’s useful as a lesson in why app ecosystems and long-term support matter.
