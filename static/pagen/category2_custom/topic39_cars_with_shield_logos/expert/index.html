FUN FACTS ABOUT SHIELD LOGOS

A few cool details behind the world’s most famous automotive shields.

Ferrari’s Pilot Tribute

The Ferrari horse comes from a WWI fighter pilot’s emblem—given to Enzo Ferrari by the pilot’s mother.

Porsche Blends Two Crests

The Porsche badge merges symbols from two German regions: Stuttgart and Württemberg.

Lamborghini’s Bull is Personal

Ferruccio Lamborghini chose the bull because it’s his zodiac sign — Taurus.
