Hotel Kabuki

Located in San Francisco’s Japantown, Hotel Kabuki offers a calm, comfortable stay—ideal for families traveling with young children. The quiet surroundings, wide sidewalks, and nearby cafés make it easy to explore during the day and unwind at night.

  • ✔ Peaceful neighborhood for restful evenings
  • ✔ Stroller-friendly streets & walkways
  • ✔ Parks, cafés, and casual dining nearby
  • ✔ Easy access to city attractions

3-Day San Francisco Family Itinerary

A relaxed, kid-friendly weekend plan featuring must-see San Francisco sights, easy family meals, and thoughtfully built-in downtime for parents and kids.

Day 1 – Waterfront & Pier 39

🍳 Breakfast

Eight AM Brunch
Relaxed, kid-friendly breakfast near Hotel Kabuki.

🌊 Morning Activities

  • Pier 39 – sea lions & waterfront views
  • Aquarium of the Bay (stroller-friendly)
  • Short walk along Fisherman’s Wharf

🍔 Lunch

Delancey Street Restaurant
Casual American food with fast service.

🚋 Afternoon

  • Ride a classic San Francisco cable car
  • Optional short Bay cruise
  • Return to hotel for rest time

🍽️ Dinner

Mo’s Grill
Simple burgers & kids’ favorites.

Day 2 – Golden Gate Park & Museums

🥞 Breakfast

Sweet Maple
Comfort brunch with kid-friendly options.

🌳 Morning Activities

  • Golden Gate Park walk
  • Koret Children’s Playground
  • California Academy of Sciences

🥪 Lunch

Park Café or Picnic
Keep it light and flexible for kids.

🎨 Afternoon

  • Children’s Creativity Museum
  • Optional hotel nap / downtime

🍝 Dinner

Emmy’s Spaghetti Shack
Casual pasta, relaxed atmosphere.

Day 3 – Science, Animals & Farewell

🍳 Breakfast

Sears Fine Food
Classic pancakes & easy kid options.

🧪 Morning Activities

  • Exploratorium interactive exhibits
  • Toddler-friendly hands-on learning

🥗 Lunch

Lapisara Eatery
Light lunch before afternoon outing.

🦁 Afternoon

  • San Francisco Zoo
  • Indoor play option if weather is cold

🍗 Dinner

The Front Porch
Comfort food for a relaxed final night.
