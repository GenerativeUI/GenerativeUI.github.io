14-Day Romantic Itinerary

A carefully crafted day-by-day guide to maximize your romantic experience across four stunning islands.

Your Romantic Journey

Week 1: Santorini & Mykonos

1-3
Days 1-3: Santorini Arrival
Oia & Fira
  • Arrive at Santorini Airport, transfer to your luxury cave hotel in Oia
  • Sunset dinner with Caldera views in Oia
  • Explore Fira, visit Museum of Prehistoric Thira
  • Catamaran sunset cruise with dinner
Recommended: Canaves Oia Suites or Katikies Hotel
4-7
Days 4-7: Mykonos Vibes
Mykonos Town & Beaches
  • Ferry to Mykonos, check into beachfront hotel
  • Explore Mykonos Town (Little Venice, windmills)
  • Beach day at Psarou or Super Paradise Beach
  • Delos Island archaeological day trip
  • Romantic dinner at a seaside taverna
Recommended: Cavo Tagoo or Bill & Coo Suites

Week 2: Crete & Naxos

8-11
Days 8-11: Cretan Adventure
Chania & Heraklion
  • Flight to Crete, explore Venetian Harbor in Chania
  • Samaria Gorge hike (moderate difficulty)
  • Visit Palace of Knossos, Heraklion Archaeological Museum
  • Wine tasting in local vineyards
  • Beach day at Elafonissi or Balos Lagoon
Recommended: Domes Noruz or Blue Palace Resort
12-14
Days 12-14: Naxos Relaxation
Naxos Town & Beaches
  • Ferry to Naxos, visit Portara (Temple of Apollo)
  • Explore mountain villages (Apeiranthos, Filoti)
  • Beach day at Agios Prokopios or Plaka Beach
  • Cooking class: Learn to make traditional Greek dishes
  • Final romantic dinner, departure on Day 15
Recommended: Naxian Collection or Kavos Hotel

Transportation Between Islands

Ferries are the most romantic way to island hop. Consider flights for longer distances.

Essential Travel Tips

Expert advice to ensure your Greek Islands honeymoon is smooth, romantic, and unforgettable.

Practical Advice

Language & Communication

English is widely spoken in tourist areas. Learning a few Greek phrases ("efharistó" for thank you) is appreciated.

Money Matters

Carry some cash (Euros) for small purchases, markets, and tips. Credit cards are widely accepted.

Weather & Clothing

Summers are hot (25-35°C). Pack light clothing, sunscreen, hats, and comfortable shoes for cobblestone streets.

Romantic Surprise Ideas

  • Arrange for champagne and flowers in your room upon arrival
  • Book a private photography session to capture your honeymoon memories
  • Surprise your partner with a sunset horseback ride in Santorini
  • Arrange a private dinner on your hotel balcony with Caldera views

Greek Dining Etiquette

Dinner is late (9-11pm). Meals are leisurely affairs meant to be enjoyed.

Health & Safety

Sun Protection

The Greek sun is intense. Use high SPF sunscreen, reapply often, and seek shade during peak hours (12-3pm).

Travel Insurance

Highly recommended! Covers medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost luggage.

COVID-19 Considerations

Check current entry requirements before travel. Some islands may have specific regulations.

Water Safety

Tap water is generally safe in cities but often tastes chlorinated. Bottled water is inexpensive and widely available.
