Detroit Red Wings Founded

1972 Islanders

New York Islanders Founded

Team Legacy & Culture

The rich traditions, nicknames, and cultural impact of both franchises

Detroit Red Wings Legacy

The Red Wings are one of the "Original Six" NHL teams, founded in 1926. Known as "Hockeytown," Detroit has one of the most passionate fan bases in professional sports.

98
Years in NHL
25
Consecutive Playoff Years

Notable Achievements:

  • Longest playoff streak in NHL history (25 consecutive seasons, 1991-2016)
  • 11 Stanley Cup Championships (most by any U.S.-based NHL team)
  • Home to "The Production Line" - legendary forward line of the 1950s
  • Joe Louis Arena - "The Joe" - historic home from 1979-2017

New York Islanders Legacy

The Islanders joined the NHL in 1972 and quickly established a dynasty, winning four consecutive Stanley Cups from 1980-1983 - one of the greatest achievements in sports history.

52

  • 19 consecutive playoff series wins (1980-1984) - NHL record
  • Nassau Coliseum - "Fort Neverlose" - historic home from 1972-2015
  • Strong defensive tradition and goaltending excellence throughout franchise history

Iconic Moments & Achievements

Historical achievements and records verified through NHL official statistics and archives

The Production Line

Gordie Howe, Sid Abel, and Ted Lindsay formed "The Production Line" - one of hockey's most legendary forward lines in the 1950s.

The Dynasty

Islanders' four consecutive Stanley Cups remain one of the most dominant runs in professional sports history.

25-Year Playoff Streak

Red Wings made the playoffs every year from 1991-2016, an unmatched consistency in modern NHL history.

19 Series Wins

Islanders won 19 consecutive playoff series from 1980-1984, an NHL record that stands today.

Hockeytown

Detroit earned the nickname "Hockeytown" for its passionate fan base and rich hockey tradition.

Fort Neverlose

Nassau Coliseum was known as "Fort Neverlose" during the Islanders' dynasty years, where they were nearly unbeatable.

The Stanley Cup

The ultimate prize in hockey - both teams have lifted this iconic trophy, creating unforgettable championship moments.
